Our Mission

Rescuing Food.
Restoring Dignity.

Together we can stop hunger. 🌾

FoodBridge is a food rescue mission that bridges the gap between surplus food and those who need it most. Every day, tonnes of edible food are discarded while millions go hungry — we believe this doesn't have to be the case.

We connect restaurants, supermarkets, and households with community members facing food insecurity. Through awareness, education, and direct action, FoodBridge builds a world where no good food goes to waste and no one goes to bed hungry.

733M People face hunger globally
1/3 Of all food produced is wasted
2.5B Tonnes wasted each year
