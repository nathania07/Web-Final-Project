30 Hours for Change

飢餓三十

When we choose to abstain from solid food for 12 or 30 hours, we are choosing to set aside our own basic needs and experience the lives of vulnerable children through action. Experiencing hunger allows us to better understand the impact of "starvation" and "scarcity" on children, leading to concrete actions.

The event is a global humanitarian relief operation initiated by World Vision, and it is also an activity with great social educational significance. Since 1989, World Vision Taiwan has been raising funds from countless Taiwanese people every year to alleviate the most urgent needs in disaster-stricken areas around the world.
